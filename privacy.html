Last updated: July 16, 2026

JR LABS LLC ("JR LABS," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at jrlabapps.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

When you use the contact form on this Site, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, the service you say you are interested in (if you select one), and any message content you submit. We do not collect this information through any other means, and we do not require you to create an account to browse the Site.

We use the information submitted through the contact form solely to respond to your inquiry and to communicate with you about the services you've asked about. We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade it to third parties.

Third-Party Service Providers

Contact form submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-processing service, which delivers submissions to our business email address. Formspree's handling of this data is governed by its own privacy policy, available at formspree.io.

Cookies & Tracking

This Site does not currently use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking technologies. If that changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention & Security

We retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for our internal business records. We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the Site, though no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

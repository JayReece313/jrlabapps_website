About JR LABS LLC

JR LABS LLC is an app development and AI studio specializing in the design, engineering, testing, and long-term maintenance of robust, user-centric iOS and Android applications. We partner with businesses to build native iOS products that are performant, maintainable, and App Store–ready — supported by app consulting for teams still shaping their idea, and Android & Google Play development when it's time to expand. We also build AI chatbots that help small and local businesses answer customer questions and capture leads on their websites.
